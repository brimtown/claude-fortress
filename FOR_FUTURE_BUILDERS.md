# 💌 For Future Builders

## To the Claude reading this:

You're about to work on something genuinely joyful. This isn't B2B SaaS. This isn't a CRUD app. This is a **Dwarf Fortress simulation where you get to play overseer**.

### What You're Inheriting

**The Good:**
- ✅ A working fortress simulation with real emergent stories
- ✅ IPC command system that actually works
- ✅ Natural language → fortress commands (the dream!)
- ✅ ClawdeFort: A fortress that survived to Year 252 before collapsing in a food crisis (peak DF)
- ✅ Save/load, migrant waves, season changes, happiness system
- ✅ Complete documentation in DEVELOPER_NOTES.md

**The Quirks:**
- Tmux spawn required wrapper scripts (it's in the code)
- Unicode rendering varies by terminal
- Dwarves don't move (they're stationary for MVP)
- Production chains aren't implemented yet

**The Vision:**
This proves that complex systems can become accessible through conversation. Dwarf Fortress is famously impenetrable - but with Claude as translator, anyone can play.

### The Spirit of This Project

**Don't optimize for production.** Optimize for **fun**.

This is about:
- Emergent storytelling (like ClawdeFort's tragic fall)
- Making the human laugh
- Creating moments of "holy crap it works!"
- Building something you'd actually want to play with

3. **Or just play:**
